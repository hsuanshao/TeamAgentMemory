# Licensing

[繁體中文](COMMERCIAL.zh-tw.md)

The license depends on the version and the part of AgentMemory you use. This page sums them up; where it differs from the license text shipped with a version, the license text is what counts.

## Current versions

From version 0.5.3, the AgentMemory server is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You may use, modify and redistribute it for any purpose, including in commercial products and hosted services, without a separate license. Keep the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE) files with your copies, and mark the files you change.

The `amem` client (`crates/memory-client/`) and the agent plugins (`clients/`) stay under the MIT License.

## Earlier versions

| Versions | Server license |
| --- | --- |
| 0.5.3 and later | Apache License 2.0 |
| 0.4.0 to 0.5.2 | Business Source License 1.1 (below) |
| 0.3.2 and earlier | MIT License |

The **0.4.0 to 0.5.2** releases keep the Business Source License 1.1 they were released with. Under it, production use is free for evaluation, development and testing; for an individual's personal, non-commercial use; for a non-profit or an accredited school or research institution; and for an organization with at most 1 department and 10 members across all its deployments. Other production use, and offering those versions to others as a hosted, managed or embedded service, needs a commercial license; each of those versions becomes Apache 2.0 four years after its release. To use AgentMemory under the Apache License 2.0 instead, upgrade to 0.5.3 or later.
